Features

Everything a shop needs, nothing it does not

Built from three years of sitting behind real counters in Qo'qon and asking what actually gets in the way.

Till

Sell in three taps: scan or search, choose cash, card or credit, done. Quick-cash buttons for 50 000, 100 000 and 200 000.

Stock by size and colour

A full matrix per product, a separate price per variant if you need it, and a warning before a size runs out.

Credit ledger

Every debt with a date and a history of payments. The app tells you who to call today, so nothing quietly disappears.

Staff, roles and shifts

A seller never sees your cost price. Open and close a shift, print a Z-report, and every action stays in the audit log.

Profit and reports

Revenue minus cost minus expenses, per day, product, seller or branch. Export to Excel whenever your accountant asks.

Branches and transfers

Move goods between shops or to the warehouse and both sides confirm it. Every report can be read per branch or for everything at once.

AI adviser

Ask in plain Uzbek which goods are stuck, what to reorder and where the price is wrong. Included in the Pro plan.

Real offline mode

Sales are written to the phone first and synced later. Changes travel as differences, so two devices offline never wipe each other's work.

Bluetooth receipts

Any 58 mm ESC/POS printer. Your shop name, the items, the total and the debt left — printed in seconds.

Barcode scanning

Use the phone camera or a USB scanner. No barcode on the item? The app prints one for you.

Notifications

A daily summary, a debt coming due, stock running low, a subscription about to end — on the phone, not buried in a menu.

Discounts and promotions

A percentage or a fixed amount, on one product or the whole basket, with a start and end date so nothing runs forever by accident.

Every table is locked to your shop id by the database itself, not by the app. A query from another shop simply returns nothing.

Stopping never deletes anything

If a subscription lapses, access pauses — the data stays exactly where it was. Pay and everything comes back untouched.

Answers

What shopkeepers ask us first

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. Every sale, delivery and customer is written to the phone first, and syncs when the connection comes back. Each change travels as a difference rather than a total — so if a seller sells three offline while the storekeeper receives five, you end up with the right number, not one of them overwritten.

Can another shop see my numbers?

No. Every table is protected at the database level and tied to your shop id. Even a request sent by hand from another account returns nothing. Your prices, customers and profit stay yours.

What if I miss a payment?

A reminder arrives 7 days before the end, then daily for the last 3. After the date the app keeps working with a banner on top. Only after about 45 days is access paused — and even then nothing is deleted. Pay and everything comes back as it was.

Do I have to buy a receipt printer?

No. The app works perfectly without one and many shops start that way. If you want printed receipts, the setup package with a 58 mm Bluetooth printer is 700 000 instead of 400 000.

My sellers are not good with phones.

That is who it was designed for. Big buttons at the bottom of the screen, plain Uzbek instead of error codes, everything reachable with one thumb, and a mistake can always be corrected. We train your team as part of the setup.

Is my old data lost?

No. Send us your Excel file, or photos of the notebook, and we move the products, prices and open debts across for you before you start.

Which shops does it suit?

Clothing and footwear first — that is where the size and colour matrix matters most. Grocery, plumbing and accessory shops work too: choose your type when you sign up and the fields change to match.
