Answers

Questions we get every week

How much will my project cost?

It depends on scope. A landing page starts at 2 000 000 UZS, a mobile app at 6 000 000, a large platform at 12 000 000. After a short call we send an exact figure and date — and nothing starts until you approve it.

How long does it take?

Landing page: 5–10 days. App or MVP: 4–8 weeks. Large platform: 8–14 weeks. Enterprise system: from two months. The exact date is fixed once the feature list is agreed.

Do you have a ready-made system for shops?

Yes — Uztomic CRM. Sales, stock by size and colour, a digital credit ledger, staff roles and profit reports. It works offline, runs on phone and Windows, and starts at 150 000 UZS a month with 14 days free.

Who owns the code and the accounts?

You do. On final payment we hand over the source code, the domain and the store accounts. You can take them to any developer afterwards.

What happens after launch?

Three months of free support: we fix bugs and answer questions. After that you can take a monthly maintenance agreement, or not — your call.

Can we work together if I'm in another region?

Yes. We work remotely across Uzbekistan and with clients abroad over Telegram, phone and video. You're welcome to visit us in Qo'qon too.

I already have an app. Can you fix or continue it?

Often yes. Send us the code or store link and we'll review it, tell you honestly what shape it's in, and quote for the work.

Not ready to fill in a form?

Send one line on Telegram or simply call. Twenty free minutes, and you leave with a number and a date — even if we never end up working together.
